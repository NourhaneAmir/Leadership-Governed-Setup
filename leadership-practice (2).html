Background & Context

The current catheterization lab equipment is 8 years old and approaching end-of-life. Increasing cardiac caseload (32% year-over-year) requires upgraded capabilities including advanced imaging, automated hemodynamic monitoring, and AI-assisted procedure planning.

Recommendation

Committee unanimously recommends Option A: Full replacement with Philips Azurion 7. ROI analysis shows payback within 3.5 years through increased procedure volume and reduced downtime. Aligns with the hospital's strategic plan for cardiac center of excellence.
